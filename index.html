About

Hey, I'm Camden! I love helping churches achieve their goals. I work full time on a creative media team in Oklahoma City, and I've been at it since 2019 (full time since 2024). I'll be honest, it's a lot, and it's worth it. The relationships you build and the stories you tell build your passion every day you show up. It's a special job.

On the side, I freelance video and build website tools for other churches. I've served on small staffs and on some of the largest, so I get what teams are up against: tight budgets, big hopes, and a Sunday that shows up every single week. Everything I make is simple, practical, and built around one goal: reaching every person, every time.
