About

Hi, I'm Camden. I work in church media here in Oklahoma City, and I've been at it since 2019, full time since 2024. I love this work, and I'm honest about it: it's a lot, and it's worth it.

I know what church teams are up against because I'm on one. Small staffs, tight budgets, big hopes. So I freelance video and build website tools that are simple and practical, made for real teams rather than a full production department.

My heart is for missions. I believe good storytelling and good tools can help a church reach people well, whether that's across town or around the world.
